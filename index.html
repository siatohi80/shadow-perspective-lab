The camera stays level, so verticals stay vertical. Angle 0° gives one-point perspective; any other angle gives two-point. Free orbit lets the camera tilt (three-point).

Plan view from above. Drag the light. The dashed line through the forms splits the ground into the viewer's side (light in front) and the far side (light behind).
